  • Each accepted paper must register with an author registration by 12 September 2026.

  • For author registrations, you must specify the paper ID.

  • The fee of additional banquet ticket is AUD 200 per person.

  • Registration for the conference cannot be canceled. There is no refund of registration fees.

  • Contact Nan Li (nanl@uow.edu.au) with subject "CANS2026 Registration" if you have any registration problem.

  • Contact the general chair Fuchun Guo (fuchun@uow.edu.au) for visa invitation letter if you don't receive it within 3 days after registration.

  • The paper acceptance email serves as the formal notification of acceptance. If you require an official acceptance letter, please contact CANS 2026 (cans2026@easychair.org).

  • Unfortunately, the conference is unable to provide financial support. Participants are expected to cover their own expenses.
