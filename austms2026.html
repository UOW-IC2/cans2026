AustMS 2026

Cryptography, Elliptic Curves and Dynamics

In the week following CANS 2026, another cryptography-focused meeting will take place in Australia: the special session Cryptography, Elliptic Curves and Dynamics at the annual conference of the Australian Mathematical Society (AustMS 2026), to be held in Perth from 1–4 December 2026.

The organisers welcome anyone interested in the topics of the special session to participate and submit an abstract to give a talk.

Session Description

Current cryptographic protocols are vulnerable to advancements in quantum computing, motivating the search for new mathematically robust approaches. Promising directions arise from elliptic curves, lattices, arithmetic dynamics, and integrable systems, where rich algebraic and dynamical structures can be exploited for cryptographic applications.

This session aims to bring together researchers working across these fields to highlight emerging connections between them. Topics include elliptic curves, lattice- and isogeny-based cryptography, homomorphic encryption, pseudorandomness, dynamical systems over finite fields, discrete integrable systems, and their computational aspects.
